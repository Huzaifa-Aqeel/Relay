# Relay Build Progress

Last updated: 2026-09-25 (Asia/Karachi)

## Source of truth

- Product requirements: `relay_functional_requirements.md`
- Product loop: Capture → Organize → Review → Handoff check → Publish → Ask
- Android first, iOS second, using Expo, Supabase, RevenueCat, Unstructured, and Astra DB.

## Current status and workflow

The completed v1.3 baseline is retained. Multi-role access, successor inheritance, Organization billing, ownership transfer, immutable publication, Ask Relay, and publication-based Organization Memory remain implemented. Capture is the user-facing contribution model: one editable Capture stores optional text, zero or more current attachments, and optional guided-prompt metadata. Voice uses Record → Stop → Transcribing → editable transcript, then places the confirmed transcript into the same Capture text path as typing; audio is never persisted. **Save only persists the latest Capture state. Organize is explicit and is the only action that parses/indexes pending files or calls Groq.** Ready unchanged files are reused; removed files are excluded and cleaned on the next Organize. File identity is exact SHA-256 only—there is no fuzzy version detection, supersedes lineage, document delta flow, or historical working-document retention. Review continues to group suggestions by Capture with exact text/attachment provenance. Organize creates context-rich entries in five broad categories, and Ask Relay uses hybrid BM25F plus publication-scoped semantic retrieval over immutable published Knowledge Items. The existing Preflight engine remains the automatic lightweight **Handoff check** after Review. The previously recorded baseline checks remain valid for their checkpoints; the 2026-09-25 Free-Role entitlement and recent UI cleanup were intentionally not test-run at the user's request and require device verification.

- One Organization Owner oversees continuity, creates Roles, manages assignments, manages purchase attachment, and offers ownership transfer. Ownership alone does not authorize Role editing or private raw-source access.
- An accepted ordinary Member is now distinct from both the Owner and a designated Role Holder. Members see the shared Organization home only; membership alone grants no Handoff, Capture, Review, Publish, Source, or Institutional Memory access. A separate active Role Assignment continues to unlock the existing exact-Role experience.
- A signed-in non-member can search limited Organization identity, submit a membership request, and see its status. Only the current Owner can accept or reject it; acceptance creates active ordinary membership without creating a Role Assignment. Existing designated-role invitation acceptance remains the path that atomically activates membership, the Role Assignment, and the correct inherited workspace.
- The Organization home is member-facing and owner-maintained: dynamic name/institution, one compact collapsible about/video section, a compact collapsible list of owner-titled private PDFs, and a collapsible committee derived from existing Roles and active assignments. Organization files use a generic list rather than hardcoded Constitution/Safety templates. No society-specific content is hardcoded. Owner editing and membership decisions live behind the existing sole-Owner check.
- The Handoff tab now lists only the signed-in user's active assigned Roles. Selecting one opens the existing Role page and its current Start/Continue Handoff behavior; organization-wide Role discovery is no longer the Handoff-tab entry point. On the Owner management page, Current committee rows are display-only, **Add role** remains the sole Role action there, and **Save organization page** follows the committee section.
- Organization Role cards are deliberately compact: Role name, current service period, and assigned holder only. Approved/unresolved counts, Handoff-check/publication labels, and lifecycle draft pills are permanently omitted from those cards; detailed continuity remains in the Role view.
- An authenticated active Role Holder maintains the workspace for their assigned Organization + Role + service period. An Owner can separately accept an assignment to maintain a Role.
- Owner can invite for any Role; the latest active Role Holder can invite the next holder of their own Role or their own mid-year replacement. The seven-day, single-use HTTPS App Link opens the installed Relay app when available and otherwise falls back to the hosted Relay page. It shows only the Organization name, Role, period, replacement status, and expiry before authentication → invitee signs in or creates an account → explicitly accepts → membership, assignment, and workspace activation happen atomically. Acceptance refreshes the Member/Role access boundary, then opens the full tabbed Role Holder workspace on **Handoff**, where the accepted Role is available; it no longer strands the invitee on a top-level detail screen without navigation. The opaque token grants no authority before authenticated acceptance.
- Same-period replacement continues the same working Handoff and preserves previous contributor attribution. A new-period successor receives a separate workspace seeded from the previous publication's approved knowledge and safe lineage/citations; private Sources and unresolved/rejected proposals are not copied.
- Maintain a living Draft throughout the term → edit and Save a Capture → explicitly Organize → Review → automatic Handoff check → resolve if needed → preview → deliberately publish for a leadership transition. Early publication and deliberate republication are supported. Reopening a published workspace leaves its existing recipient snapshot unchanged until republication.
- An Incoming Recipient reads and asks questions through a published link without logging in. That link grants no membership or Role Assignment.
- A human Purchaser's verified annual RevenueCat entitlement benefits one Organization and its authorized Role Holders. Only the current Owner can initiate/attach a purchase. Authorization and Organization plan checks are separate; ownership transfer preserves the subscription association, paid-through date, renewal state, and purchaser identity without transferring the purchaser's store account. Canceling renewal keeps Organization Pro active until verified expiry; an incoming Owner can buy a later annual term after expiry but cannot attach an overlapping purchase while the Organization is already Pro.

Output is independently validated, restricted to the five broad categories for new suggestions, required to carry an exact source excerpt, written atomically with provenance, and always enters the human queue as Proposed (`AI-01`–`AI-04`, `KNOW-01`–`KNOW-05`).

Safe source labels/locators are snapshotted for recipient citations (`HAND-04`, `PUB-02`, `PUB-04`, `PUB-06`, `SEC-01`, `SEC-02`).

Field-aware BM25F and Astra semantic ranking over published `title + content` are fused with deterministic RRF (`k=60`). - Ask uses strict-schema `answered` / `unsupported` / `conflict` results. Unsupported answers are normalized to the fixed refusal; material conflict output requires an explicit conflict flag and at least two valid published evidence refs, explains the disagreement without selecting a winner, and renders both citations to the recipient.

Billing refresh, purchase, and restore also invalidate the continuity response so Role locks update with the verified plan instead of remaining in client cache (`MON-07`, `MON-12`, `MON-14`, `MON-17`).
- Ask allowances are claimed atomically without database polling: 10 requests per published Handoff/day by default for Free and 100 for an associated Pro Organization, both server-configurable and bounded against public-link abuse.
- Draft Handoffs remain living workspaces throughout a service period. The Handoff page has one compact Capture launcher and one focused composer supporting typed text, confirmed voice transcript, and multiple file attachments, with optional fixed prompts for responsibilities, registration/training, finances, recurring events, contacts, access, deadlines, policies, and lessons (`HAND-05`, `CAP-01`–`CAP-10`). No calendar, reminder, notification, or task-management system was added.
- One editable Capture contains an editable title, optional user text, optional `prompt_id`, and zero or more current attachments. A guided Capture pre-fills the title from its chip label and a custom Capture starts with a blank title; either title can be changed by the user. If the title is left blank, Relay retains its existing concise fallback derived from the prompt, current text, or attachment names. Capture text has one private internal evidence Source for exact provenance, while every current attachment remains its own document Source for extraction, Astra, and citations. `capture_sources` is the direct relation; no capture-group/session/orchestration layer exists.
- A selected prompt keeps its exact guiding question visible and stores only its prompt identifier. It is never evidence and cannot force Source meaning, Knowledge Item type, or proposal classification. Custom and guided Captures use the same composer and backend path.
- Save creates/updates the Capture, internal text evidence, and current attachment links only. New files are privately uploaded as `pending`; Save never parses, indexes, calls Groq, or creates proposals. Explicit Organize cleans removed attachments, processes only pending/failed current files, reuses current ready files, waits for all current files to become ready, then makes one concurrency-guarded Groq structured call over the Role, current Capture text, current attachment content, and approved knowledge. It atomically writes zero or more reviewable create/update/retire suggestions and never auto-approves.
- Review suggestions carry `capture_id` for user-facing grouping while every suggestion still cites one exact internal evidence Source and a validated contiguous excerpt/locator. Review uses compact typed cards, current-versus-suggested layouts for updates/retirements, collapsed **View support** provenance, action-specific approval labels, and immediate decision feedback; the full approved-knowledge list no longer competes with the review task. **Don't add** rejects immediately on web and native without a confirmation dialog, while **Add to handoff** calls the guarded approval RPC directly. With no suggestions, Review replaces the former summary/banner/dashed-card combination with one concise empty state and Return to Capture action; rejecting every suggestion in the current Review session shows the distinct **Everything reviewed** message. Capture cards derive their displayed review state from live pending suggestions: the count decreases as decisions are made and the status disappears when none remain, rather than continuing to show the historical Organize result. The main Handoff history shows Captures and can expand attachment details; it does not expose the internal text Source or list every attachment as a separate primary capture.
- Capture Organize considers current approved knowledge and can create reviewable `update` or `retire` suggestions that show the existing item, proposed replacement, and exact new evidence. Accepting an update changes the existing canonical Knowledge Item atomically, retains its lineage and prior revision, and copies the new provenance; rejecting remains non-mutating.
- Organize keeps proposal granularity successor-focused: facts are grouped into an operational unit before one primary broad category is assigned. Steps, task-specific contacts, thresholds, explicit warnings/reasons, examples, and historical context are consolidated into the parent entry when they would not be useful alone. A boundary-and-coverage audit suppresses cross-category duplicates without omitting grounded guidance; unrelated workflows remain separate and every consolidated claim retains exact Source provenance.
- **Add files** reads the selected bytes and calculates SHA-256 immediately. If the same hash already exists in the Handoff—or is already selected in the open composer—the duplicate is rejected before it enters the attachment list or uploads. Save repeats the same hash check as a race-condition safeguard. Different bytes are a different attachment; filename heuristics and semantic/fuzzy version matching have been removed.
- XLSX remains on the existing Unstructured ingestion path. Removed attachments are marked on Save, excluded from current Capture reads immediately, and have stale Astra/Storage/Source material cleaned by the next Organize when no active Capture still references them. There is no working document history, source-version metadata, or delta Organize path.
- The private `handoff-sources` Storage allow-list explicitly includes XLSX again. Capture, Storage, and the existing Unstructured processor now agree on XLSX support; unsupported Storage MIME failures also produce a specific user-facing file-type message rather than the generic request error.
- Working Preflight document retrieval filters to active current Capture attachment IDs. Published Ask Relay uses only its immutable published Knowledge Item snapshot and never treats working files as recipient answer evidence.
- Approved Knowledge Items now carry durable Role-scoped lineage. Publications snapshot the lineage, accepted same-Handoff updates retain it, and strong same-type/topic year-to-year matches can connect equivalent knowledge without forcing uncertain matches. Canonical revision and proposal decision history is preserved.
- Organization Memory preserves and compares only immutable published approved Knowledge Item snapshots from outgoing leaders for adjacent service periods of the same Organization + Role. Working Captures, file changes, hashes, removed attachments, and Astra chunks are never Organization Memory inputs. Its What Changed view shows conservative Added/Changed/Retired material changes, before/after approved snapshots, safe published source labels/locators, and no scores, rankings, cross-role analytics, or claims of improvement.
- Change reasons are restricted to policy-driven, lesson-driven, leadership preference, contact/resource change, or `Unknown / not established`. Policy and lesson causality require explicit approved evidence; chronology alone is rejected, leadership preference requires an explicit decision statement, and contact/resource classification requires that knowledge type. Authorized Owner/Role Holder confirmation is attributable, policy cannot be confirmed without policy evidence, and lesson confirmation requires selecting an approved Lesson plus a resulting Process/Warning/Responsibility, creating an explicit lesson-to-practice relationship.
- Recipient Start Here now derives a compact immediate-transition section only from approved immutable publication items, covering surfaced registration/training, finance closeout, important introductions, deadlines, and account/tool access instructions without inventing tasks or exposing draft sources (`REC-06`).
