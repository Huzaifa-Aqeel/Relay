# Relay

Student organizations lose practical knowledge every time leadership changes. Important deadlines, event procedures, useful contacts, unfinished work, and lessons learned are often scattered across documents, chats, spreadsheets, and conversations.

Relay turns that material into a reviewed, role-specific handoff for the next leader.

## How Relay works

**Capture → Organize → Review → Publish → Ask → Remember**

1. **Capture**

   The outgoing leader adds notes, voice recordings, and supporting files in one place.

2. **Organize**

   Relay identifies useful operational knowledge such as procedures, deadlines, contacts, resources, warnings, and unfinished obligations. It keeps supporting source references with each suggestion.

3. **Review**

   The Role Holder approves, edits, or rejects every suggestion. Nothing becomes official handoff knowledge without human review.

4. **Publish**

   Relay creates an immutable handoff for that Organization, Role, and service period. Published handoffs remain available in History.

5. **Ask Relay**

   The next Role Holder can ask questions about the previous published handoff and receive answers grounded in approved knowledge and its sources.

6. **Organization Memory**

   Once the same Role has another published handoff, Relay shows what materially changed since the previous service period: **Added**, **Changed**, and **Retired** knowledge.

## Built for real leadership transitions

Relay accepts the mixed material student leaders already use:

- transition notes and role guides
- event plans and checklists
- calendars and annual timelines
- budgets and financial procedures
- policies, constitutions, and forms
- contact lists and resource directories
- meeting notes and lessons learned
- voice explanations and typed context

Relay does not simply summarize a folder. It extracts independently useful knowledge that the next person can act on.

## Role succession

The current Role Holder can invite the next person taking over that same Role.

When the incoming leader accepts:

- the outgoing assignment ends
- the incoming leader becomes the active Role Holder
- the incoming leader receives a clean new handoff draft
- previous published handoffs remain unchanged in History
- the incoming leader can consult the previous handoff through Ask Relay

The Organization Owner can also manage Role assignments when administrative recovery is needed. This prevents a Role from being stranded if its current holder becomes unavailable.

## Relay Plus

Relay Plus is an annual organization plan managed through RevenueCat.

- The Organization Owner purchases or restores the plan for a selected Organization.
- Plus supports multiple active Roles, organization-wide handoff history, Organization Memory, continuity between service periods, and larger document and Ask Relay allowances.
- Subscription status is verified by Relay's backend and kept current through RevenueCat.
- Canceling renewal does not immediately remove access; Plus remains active through the paid period.
- Existing knowledge and published handoffs are preserved if the Organization returns to the Free plan.

## Trust and control

- AI suggestions always require human review.
- Published handoffs are immutable historical records.
- Answers and knowledge retain safe source provenance.
- Raw captures and working documents are not exposed as published knowledge.
- Access is scoped by Organization and Role.
- Database authorization protects organization data and paid capabilities.
- Relay does not rank leaders or claim that one service period was better than another.

## Technology

Relay is built with Expo, React Native, TypeScript, Supabase, Unstructured, Astra DB, RevenueCat, and provider-neutral language-model integration.
